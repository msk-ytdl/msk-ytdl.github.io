How to Install & Run

Simple, easy-to-follow steps to get started in less than 60 seconds.

01

Download the APK

Tap the Download APK button above to download MSK-VidMate.apk to your Android device.

02

Allow Unknown Apps

When prompted by Chrome or your File Manager, tap Settings and enable "Allow from this source".

03

Tap Install

Click Install. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap "Install anyway" (it is safe and open source).

04

Share to Download

Open the app directly, or in the YouTube app tap Share → MSK VidMate for instant 1-tap download!

01

Download ZIP

Download MSK-YTDL-Windows-x64.zip from the download center or GitHub Releases.

02

Extract the ZIP

Right-click the zip file and select Extract All... to any folder on your computer.

03

Launch the App

Double-click MSK-YTDL.exe or Start_MSK_YTDL.bat to launch the interactive terminal or GUI.

04

SmartScreen Tip

If Windows Defender displays "Windows protected your PC", click More info and select Run anyway.
